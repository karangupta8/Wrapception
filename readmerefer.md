# **Wrapception**

**Wrapception** is a web app that lets users upload year-end “Wrapped / Year in Review” summaries from different services (images, PDFs, text) and uses AI to generate a single unified, intelligent view of their year.

### *One Year. One Life. One Intelligence Layer.*

---

## 🧠 Problem

Every year, dozens of apps generate **beautiful but siloed** “Year in Review” summaries:

- Music apps tell you what you listened to
- Fitness apps show miles and calories
- Reading apps show books and pages
- AI tools show… nothing meaningful yet

These insights are:

- Fragmented across platforms
- Locked inside screenshots, stories, and PDFs
- Impossible to compare, correlate, or reflect on holistically

> You don’t live your life in apps.
> 
> 
> But your data does.
> 

---

## 💡 Solution

**Unified Wrapped** is a web app that lets users **upload Year-End summaries (images, PDFs, text)** from all their services and uses **AI to generate a single, intelligent, cross-domain annual view of their life.**

Instead of 15 different “Wrapped” experiences, users get:

- One unified dashboard
- One coherent narrative
- One memory they actually own

---

## 🔁 How It Works (Simple & Powerful)

1. **Upload**
    - Screenshots (Spotify Wrapped, Strava Year in Sport, Goodreads Review, etc.)
    - PDFs, exports, or copied text
2. **AI Extraction**
    - Vision + OCR + semantic parsing
    - Metrics normalized into a unified schema
3. **Intelligence Layer**
    - Cross-domain analysis
    - Patterns, correlations, trends
    - Natural-language yearly narrative
4. **Unified Output**
    - Visual dashboard
    - AI-written “Your Year” story
    - Shareable & exportable insights

---

## 📊 What Users Get (Beyond Any Wrapped)

- **Life Allocation View**
    
    Where did your attention actually go?
    
- **Behavioral Patterns**
    - Exploration vs repetition
    - Consistency vs spikes
    - Creation vs consumption
- **Cross-Domain Insights**
    
    > “Your fitness dipped during months of high coding intensity.”
    > 
    > 
    > “Your music taste narrowed while reading diversity increased.”
    > 
- **Yearly Narrative**
    
    A human-readable story of your year — not just stats.
